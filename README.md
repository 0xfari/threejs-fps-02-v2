Emberline: a round-based tactical FPS in a dusk industrial yard, built on three.js. Two teams fight short rounds — plant the spike at Site A or B, or eliminate the other team — with money earned each round, a buy menu, four guns, HE and smoke grenades, and bots that hold angles, peek from cover, fall back when hurt, react to gunfire, and smoke the site as they move in to plant. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best three.js tactical FPS" bounty, $1,000 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — click to play. WASD to move, mouse to look, click to fire, R to reload, B to buy, G for a grenade, H for smoke, E to plant or defuse the spike. First to 5 rounds takes the match.
